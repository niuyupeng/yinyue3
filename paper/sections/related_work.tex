\section{Related Work}

Early neural harmonization systems such as HARMONET explored chorale-style harmonization with connectionist methods \cite{hild1992harmonet}. DeepBach introduced a strong pseudo-Gibbs approach for Bach chorale generation \cite{hadjeres2017deepbach}, while Coconet framed counterpoint and harmonization as convolutional infilling \cite{huang2017coconet}. The present implementation follows this line of symbolic, score-level modeling, while adding explicit reports for common-practice rule violations.

The dataset and parsing utilities rely on music21, a toolkit for computer-aided musicology and symbolic score processing \cite{cuthbert2010music21}. The broader neural symbolic-music generation context is covered in recent book-length surveys of deep-learning music generation methods \cite{briot2020deep}.
