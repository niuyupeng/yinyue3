\section{Conclusion}

We present a runnable framework for explainable score-level SATB choral harmonization. The system integrates music21 chorale parsing, symbolic pitch prediction, automatic harmonic-label extraction, common-practice rule checking, rule-guided decoding, MusicXML export, result logging, table and figure generation, and materials for blinded expert evaluation. In the completed RTX 4060 Ti runs, the proposed neural-symbolic Transformer improved pitch accuracy and cross entropy relative to the vanilla/no-constraints Transformer and reduced several targeted rule violations. The same experiments show that aggregate rule counts and parallel octaves remain unresolved. The next empirical step is to collect blinded expert ratings and to replace local repair with a decoding strategy that can reason over conflicting harmony and counterpoint constraints.
