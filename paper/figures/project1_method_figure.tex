\caption{Neural-symbolic SATB harmonization pipeline. The system accepts a soprano line, bass line, partially masked SATB score, or optional Roman-numeral plan; converts the score into symbolic tokens; predicts missing voices with a Transformer; applies conservative rule-guided decoding; and exports SATB MusicXML together with an explainable rule report. Roman-numeral and chord labels are automatically estimated when available, and uncertain labels are preserved as \texttt{UNKNOWN} rather than treated as ground truth.}
